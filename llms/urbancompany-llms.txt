# Urban Company

> Urban Company (formerly UrbanClap) is an on-demand home services marketplace that connects customers with trained, hand-picked service professionals for beauty and salon services, cleaning, appliance repair, plumbing, electrical work, painting, and pest control. The platform operates across 40+ cities in India as well as the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Singapore. Urban Company does not publish a public developer API.

Urban Company is a consumer platform delivered through its website and mobile apps. As of 2026-07-21 no public developer portal, API documentation, OpenAPI specification, or client SDKs were found.
